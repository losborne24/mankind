# Temple of Portunus exterior interpretation v1

All model geometry and materials were authored for Mankind with
`scripts/reconstructions/portunus/generate.py`. No third-party mesh, photograph
texture, scanned sculpture or generic Roman-kit building was imported. The model
is project-authored content; repository terms apply. No new public licence for
the project is granted by this file.

Geometric reference: Banister Fletcher, *A History of Architecture*, sixth
edition, 1921, p.105; historic plan, front elevation and side elevation held by
Penn State University Libraries, record WB2007-0510, courtesy Allan Kohl.
The [holding library's record](https://www.flickr.com/photos/psulibscollections/5832798550/)
expressly describes this historic illustration as public-domain material available
for all uses. No image pixels are used in the model or its materials. The library
also cautions that historic reconstruction drawings contain subjective interpretation.

The [Rome heritage authority](https://soprintendenzaspecialeroma.it/luoghi/tempio-di-portunus/)
supports the building identity, Ionic order, frontal column arrangement and
Republican rebuilding context. The [city tourism record](https://www.turismoroma.it/en/places/temple-portunus)
supplies the geographic discovery point and describes the podium and side columns.
Their images and text are not redistributed as model textures or plans.

Printed dimensions are converted from feet to metres; spacing and other proportions
are inferred from the historic diagram. This is not a modern measured survey or
certified archaeological restitution. Simplified capitals, neutral finishes, roof
covering, door details, step count and terrain contacts are interpretive. Interior
fittings, sculpture and original painted decoration are omitted. No precise
appearance at an exact historical date is asserted.

The AD 100 preview is an editorial context for reviewing the classical exterior,
not a construction date or a source-attested snapshot. It remains approximate and
requires explicit selection of the interpretation. Orientation/terrain placement
are display inferences, not georeferenced survey geometry.

Model frame: metres, glTF Y-up, base Y=0, entrance +Z before placement. The normal
asset preparation centres horizontal bounds. Versioned file: `portunus-v1.glb`.
The catalogue pins the file SHA-256 and renderer registry ID. No lights, cameras,
animations, image textures or villagers are contained in the GLB.
