# Temple of Hercules Victor exterior interpretation v1

All geometry and materials were authored for Mankind with
`scripts/reconstructions/hercules-victor/generate.py`, run with Blender in
background mode. No third-party mesh, photograph, scanned sculpture or image
texture was imported. The model is project-authored content; repository terms
apply and no new public licence is granted by this file.

Dimensions: overall diameter 14.8 m and column height 10.65 m, 20 Corinthian
columns and a five-step tufa base, from
[Wikipedia (citing Loar 2017)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Temple_of_Hercules_Victor)
and [World History Encyclopedia](https://www.worldhistory.org/article/625/temple-of-vestahercules-rome/).
These are secondary sources; no survey was consulted.

Plan ratios (cella to colonnade about 0.645, wall about 0.073) were measured by eye
from Palladio, *I quattro libri dell'architettura* (Venice, 1570), Book IV p. 91, in the
[Getty Research Institute scan](https://archive.org/details/qvattrolibridel00pall/)
on the Internet Archive. The 1570 plate is public domain by age; the scan states no
licence, so only measurements are used and no pixels are redistributed. Palladio draws
about 12 columns and a dome. Both are rejected: the standing building has 20 columns
and the original roof is lost.

Inferred: column base diameter, ring radius, cella size, step rise/tread, door,
entablature, the low conical tiled roof and capital detail. Omitted: interior,
cult statue, sculpture, medieval windows and brick wall, Luna-marble column
replacements. This is an interpretation, not a restitution.
