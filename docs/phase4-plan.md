reconstruction, with the asset stored for reuse. This plan supersedes the earlier
recommendation to build another guided journey immediately after scene resolution.

Steps 7 and 8 are one bounded catalogue-plus-building milestone. Keep the catalogue
small enough to support the first building. Source suitability determines the site;
Portunus supplies the first building; Housesteads and Menander remain sourced map context.

Completion requires a visible evidenced building, inspectable sources/uncertainty/
rights/version, demonstrated catalogue reuse and a guided journey using the asset,
with reliable rendering and interaction. A property extent alone is insufficient.

Phase 5 adds AI research, automatic acquisition and scenario planning to the
working process established here. No AI research service or automatic model
acquisition has been implemented by the Phase 4 catalogue milestone.
